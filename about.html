FAQ

Good to know

How do I place an order?

Add products to your bag, tap “Place Order on WhatsApp”, fill in your delivery details and send the pre-filled message. We confirm your order personally.

Should I patch test first?

Yes — as with any skincare product, we recommend a patch test before first use. Stop use if irritation occurs.

Which products can I use together?

See our Routine page for a suggested morning and night order. If you have sensitive skin or a skin condition, consult a dermatologist first.

How is payment & delivery handled?

Delivery and payment options are confirmed with you directly on WhatsApp after you send your order.
